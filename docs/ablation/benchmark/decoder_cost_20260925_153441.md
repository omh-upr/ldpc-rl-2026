- Latency is wall clock per frame, one frame at a time, through the evaluation loop without metric collection. Decision time is the part spent choosing actions; the remainder is the environment's bookkeeping, which every decoder pays alike.
- Operations are estimates from the network's layer shapes and the measured decisions and min-sum iterations per frame (19 operations per edge per iteration; see the constants at the top of the script). The environment adds about 33 operations per flip for every decoder and is not included.
