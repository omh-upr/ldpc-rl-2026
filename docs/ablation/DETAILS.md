## 1. Success and exact recovery

Success is STOP on a zero syndrome within the flip budget. Exact recovery is a final word equal to the transmitted one, whether or not the decoder stopped. With $d_{\min} = 14$, error weight at most 6 and flip budget 7, a success is exact recovery by construction, so the first count must be zero. The second is the frames corrected without a STOP, typically on the last permitted step. Counts summed over seeds.

## 7. Resolution of a single run

How large a difference one training run can detect. *s* is the standard deviation of held-out RL success over the seeds: the noise a single run carries. Two single runs, one per configuration, then differ by seed noise alone with standard deviation about √2·s, if their seed effects are independent; the second table checks that against contrasts actually measured. *Resolution* is 1.96·√2·s, the smallest difference that seed noise alone exceeds in fewer than 5% of such pairs. *Seeds for δ* is the number of seeds per arm at which the 95% interval of the mean difference narrows to ±δ, (1.96·√2·s/δ)² rounded up; it is a normal approximation, and with Student t and few seeds the number is larger. δ = 1.43 is the gain of the re-flip mask in the development chain (`DEVELOPMENT_CHAIN.json`), δ = 0.2 that of the uniform epsilon schedule.
